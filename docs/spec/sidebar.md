# Sidebar

Search, the environments block, and drag to reorder. Part of the [Restly spec](README.md).

## D18 Sidebar editing

Search filters requests by name and URL, arrow keys walk the filtered rows, and Enter in the search box opens the first match. Environments sit in their own block pinned to the bottom of the sidebar, with their own scroll. Requests and folders can be duplicated, and dragged to reorder or move between folders and collections. Open tabs follow the moved items by identity.
