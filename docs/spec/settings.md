# Settings page

The Settings tab and its sections. Part of the [Restly spec](README.md).

## D33 Settings page

Settings is one tab with a section rail: General (auto-save, editor line wrap, and history length, first because it holds the settings that change what Restly does to files), Appearance (theme, fonts, and density), Network (proxy, User-Agent, TLS, and client certificates, with its own Save button because saving rebuilds the transports), Keyboard (opens `keybindings.json`, and lists every shortcut with a filter, marking customized ones), and About (version, build, and Show log file). General and Appearance apply immediately (2026-09-15, sections extended 2026-10-04).
